Features

Everything your family needs to stay organized and connected.

✅

Chore Tracking

Create one-time or recurring chores, assign them to family members, set due dates, and track completion. Kids earn points for every chore they finish — turning "clean your room" from a fight into a game.

⭐

Rewards System

Define your own rewards — extra screen time, a special outing, cold hard cash. Kids redeem points they've earned from chores. Parents approve or deny. You're in control.

🍽️

Meal Planning

Plan breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the week. Save your family's favorite recipes. Generate shopping lists from your meal plan with one click. No more "what's for dinner?" at 5pm.

🛒

Shopping Lists

Shared lists that everyone in the family can add to. Syncs in real time. Auto-generate from meal plans. Mark items off as you shop.

📅

Family Calendar

One shared calendar for the whole family. Sports practices, doctor appointments, school events, birthdays — all in one place. Color-coded by family member.

📚

School Tracking

Track homework assignments, projects, tests, and grades for each child. Set up subjects, add work, and see what's due this week at a glance.

🔔

Real-Time Notifications

Get notified when chores are done, rewards are redeemed, or someone adds to the shopping list. Everyone stays in the loop without group texts.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Management

Invite family members with a simple code. Set roles — parents manage, kids participate. See everyone's activity and points in one dashboard.
