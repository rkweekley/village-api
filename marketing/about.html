About Village

Built by a family, for families.

Village was born from a simple frustration: keeping a family organized shouldn't require six different apps, a refrigerator covered in sticky notes, and a group text thread that never ends.

We built Village to bring everything together — chores, meals, rewards, calendars, school tracking, and shopping lists — in one place where the whole family can see what's going on and pitch in.

The name comes from the old saying: "It takes a village to raise a child." We believe it takes a village to raise a family, too. Our app helps your village — your family — work together, celebrate wins, and stay connected.

Village is proudly built and supported in the United States. We're a small team of parents and engineers who use the product every day in our own homes. If something's not working for your family, we want to hear about it.
