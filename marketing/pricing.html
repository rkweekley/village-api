Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

Sign up and get full access to Village for 30 days. A credit card is required to start your trial, but you won't be charged until the 30 days are up. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you'll never be billed.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes. You can switch plans anytime from your Family settings or through the Stripe customer portal. Changes take effect at your next billing date.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel from your Family settings with one click. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period — we never cut you off mid-cycle.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never share or sell your family's information.
